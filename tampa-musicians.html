The Tampa Roster

Hand-picked. Auditioned. Local.

Every musician on the Tampa roster lives within thirty miles of the city. They rehearse together monthly, play together weekly, and have logged hundreds of weddings between them. When you book Luxe Harmony for a Tampa wedding, this is who shows up.

  • Violin · Lead

    Elena Marchetti

    Lead Violinist · Tampa Roster Captain

    A USF School of Music alumna, Elena has led Luxe Harmony's Tampa ensemble since 2019. She has performed alongside Joshua Bell and the Tampa Bay Symphony, and has played first violin on over 250 Florida weddings.

    • USF School of Music, BM & MM
    • Tampa Bay Symphony · Section Violinist
    • Specialties: Bach, custom arrangements, processional
  • Cello

    Marcus Reyes

    Principal Cello

    Marcus joined the Tampa roster after a six-year tenure with the Florida Orchestra. His low end gives our quartets their unmistakable warmth — and his improvised continuo lines have stopped more than one bride mid-aisle.

    • The Juilliard School, Pre-College
    • Florida Orchestra · Former Section Cellist
    • Specialties: Sacred classical, jazz, Latin
  • Violin · Second

    Sofia Park

    Second Violin

    Sofia teaches violin at Patel Conservatory by day and plays Luxe Harmony quartets by night. She has the rare gift of perfect pitch — and the rarer one of knowing when not to use it.

    • Patel Conservatory · Lead Violin Instructor
    • Eastman School of Music, BM
    • Specialties: Contemporary pop arrangements, Korean wedding music
  • Viola

    Yara Bashir

    Viola

    Yara holds the inner-voice middle of our quartets together — the glue line you don't notice until it's gone. She also writes most of our custom arrangements for first dances.

    • New England Conservatory, MM
    • 20+ custom first-dance arrangements per year
    • Specialties: Arabic classical, film scores
  • Harp

    Anneliese Voss

    Harp · Solo & Ensemble

    Anneliese is the Tampa Bay area's most-requested wedding harpist. Her solo prelude sets are the reason ceremonies start on time — guests arrive early so they don't miss them.

    • Curtis Institute of Music, BM

  • Piano · Jazz

    Daniel Okafor

    Pianist · Jazz Trio Lead

    Daniel leads our Tampa jazz trio for cocktail hours and receptions. Trained at Berklee, he has played the standards a thousand times — and still makes every one sound like the first.

    • Berklee College of Music, BM Jazz Performance
    • 15+ years professional cocktail piano
    • Specialties: Great American Songbook, Brazilian jazz, soul
Our Audition Standard

Three criteria. No exceptions.

Every musician on our Tampa roster auditioned in-person against an industry-standard panel. We don't hire by résumé — we hire by ear. And we re-audition every player annually to make sure the standard never slips.

  • Tone — the warmth, clarity, and emotional depth of the sound
  • Sight-reading — to handle custom arrangements added with grace
  • Musicality — the phrasing, taste, and feel that turn notes into something you remember
