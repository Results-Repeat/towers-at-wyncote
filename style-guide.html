A Note from the Founder

Music written for the moments that matter most.

Founded in 2012 by violinist Krystle, Luxe Harmony has grown into a curated network of conservatory-trained string musicians and jazz artists — Grammy nominees, symphony members, and educators who have performed alongside Andrea Bocelli and Michael Bublé, and on stages from Carnegie Hall to St. Peter's Basilica.
