Our Story

Music written for the moments that matter most.

Founded in 2012 by violinist Krystle, Luxe Harmony has grown into a curated network of conservatory-trained string musicians and jazz artists — Grammy nominees, symphony members, and educators who have performed alongside Andrea Bocelli, Michael Bublé, and Dave Matthews Band.

Music Programs

Crafted for every part of your celebration.

From the first guest's arrival to the last dance, every moment deserves its own melody.

  • The Ceremony

    Prelude, processional, vows, and recessional — softly woven classical and contemporary arrangements that carry your guests through every word.

  • Cocktail Hour

    Elegant strings and jazz standards between vows and reception, setting a sophisticated, conversational mood.

  • Reception & Dinner

    From soft instrumental dinner sets to lively jazz arrangements, our musicians elevate the entire evening — first dance and beyond.
