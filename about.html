About

The story of Luxe Harmony.

A network of conservatory-trained artists devoted to the craft of wedding music.

Founded 2012 · San Antonio

From a single violin to an award-winning ensemble.

Luxe Harmony began as the Lotus String Quartet, founded by violinist Krystle with a singular goal: to create unforgettable moments for music lovers. Fourteen years later, Luxe Harmony has grown into a network of conservatory-trained professionals — performing at the most refined weddings and private events across Florida, Texas, and beyond.

In 2025, we were honored as BookLive's Best Musician. But what we love most is far quieter: the held breath as a bride walks down the aisle, the soft swell of cello during a first kiss.
